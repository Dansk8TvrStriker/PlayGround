Two friends

Mira is quick and light. She can double jump, and she can coax old machines back to life at glowing terminals.

Teo is steady and strong. He can heave rubble aside, and his patched suit lets him walk through the hot places.

Press Q anywhere to switch. Some doors only open for one of them.
